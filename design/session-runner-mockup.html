What's being decided here

Real data from home-training-v1.md on every screen. Tap through with the buttons above.

Settled
Effort is three keys, and it commits the set

Reps and load pre-fill from last time, so the usual case is one tap at thumb height — no save button. RIR stays supported as an optional metric the contract can declare, but it never sits in the hot path.

Settled
Load is total kilos

The floor press reads 12 kg, not "6 kg each" — with 2 × 6 underneath as a quiet check. Goblet squat and one-arm row are single-dumbbell, so their total is 6 kg. One number that means the same thing in the charts and the export.

Settled
Rest runs itself, where the plan says so

It starts on its own and names what's coming, so you never hunt for it. It appears only where the contract declares a rest — the warm-up and the ab finisher declare none, so you just carry on. Add 30 s or start early at any point.

Layout
A list, with one exercise open

Collapsed rows keep the sequence in view; the open one gets the space and the numbers. You always know where you are without losing what's next.

Colour
Green, amber and red mean symptoms

They're reserved for the plan's pain-response framework and used nowhere decorative. That's why Easy / Medium / Hard is a fill level, not a traffic light — "Hard" is a good outcome in training, and red would say the opposite.

Hard cases
The awkward primitives, drawn

Check-off warm-ups are pills with no set rows. Per-side gets a row each side. Ranged sets draw the minimum and offer the optional one. Rounds get a progress bar, not a repeated list. Conditional exercises put the condition and both declared substitutes in front of you.

Settled
The load dial steps 1 kg

No per-configuration increment, no new contract field. On a paired lift an odd total shows as 2 × 5.5, which is exactly what 1.25 kg plates give you — so the awkward-looking number is the honest one.

Settled
The warm-up stays where it is

Six pills, quick to tick, no collapse. Small enough not to be in the way, and visible enough that it doesn't quietly stop happening.

Next
None of this gets built first

Phase 1 is the round-trip core with no UI at all — parser, diff, export, and the golden test proving an import survives a full loop with every exercise ID and the context intact. The session runner is phase 4, and it's built on that guarantee.
